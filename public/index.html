Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026 · Applies to the Latte website, web app, desktop app and mobile apps

The short version

  • Latte is a study planner for students aged 18 and over. Café Vanille runs it and is responsible for your data.
  • We collect what we need to run Latte: your account, the things you create (events, tasks, notes, chats, polls, files), payment status and technical data.
  • We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use your content to train AI models.
  • Your school does not get your personal content. If your school buys a licence, it sees only aggregated statistics for groups of 5 or more students.
  • You can delete your account yourself at any time. Deletion runs across all our services, and backups are fully purged within 7 days. To get a copy of your data, email us.

1. Who we are

Latte (latte.study) is operated by Café Vanille (“we”, “us”, “our”). For the personal data described in this policy, we are the controller under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the responsible party under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

Contact for anything about privacy: privacy@latte.study. You can also write to legal@latte.study. We have not appointed a data protection officer, because the law does not require one for our activities. Privacy requests go straight to the people who run Latte.

2. Who this policy is for

This policy applies to people who use Latte themselves: visitors, registered users and buyers of a Semester Pass. It also covers staff who sign in to the school-administration portal.

Latte is meant only for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account, and we ask you to confirm this when you sign up. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will close it and delete its data. If you think this has happened, please contact us.

5. AI features

Some Latte features use AI models. These include the Garçon study assistant, smart task ranking, course thumbnails, and search across your own groups, calendar, notes and tasks. When you use them, the relevant part of your own content is sent to models hosted on Microsoft Azure (Azure OpenAI / Azure AI Foundry) to produce an answer. To make search possible, we store searchable representations (“embeddings”) of your content in our own database. When you delete the original content, its embedding is deleted too.

  • We do not use your content to train AI models, and our AI provider does not use it to train its models either.
  • We read timetables and syllabi you import with text-recognition software that runs on our own servers.
  • AI answers can be wrong. Always check anything important.

6. Who we share data with

We share personal data only as described here. We do not sell personal data, we do not “share” it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we show no ads.

Other users

The content you post in groups, chats, shared notes and polls is visible to the people you share it with. Your name and profile picture appear next to it. Notes shared in class groups can be set to be visible to students in other groups (cohorts) of the same course. You can change this setting for each note.

Schools

Self-registered students are our users, not the school’s. If your school buys a licence, we use your verified school email to grant you its benefits. Authorised school staff see only aggregated statistics for groups of at least 5 students. They never see your messages, notes, tasks or calendar.

7. Google user data

If you sign in with Google or connect your Google Calendar to Latte, we access the following Google user data. We access it only after you grant permission on Google’s consent screen:

  • Basic profile (OpenID, name, email address): used to create your Latte account and sign you in.
  • Google Calendar, read access (calendar.readonly): used to import your existing Google Calendar events and show them in your Latte calendar, so that you can plan your studies around them.
  • Google Calendar events, write access (calendar.events): used only if you turn on two-way sync. Latte uses it to create and update, in your Google Calendar, the events you create or edit in Latte.

We use Google user data only to provide and improve these user-facing calendar and sign-in features. We do not sell Google user data. We do not use or transfer it for advertising, including personalised, retargeted or interest-based advertising. We do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes, and we do not use it to develop, improve or train generalised artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

We do not allow humans to read Google user data, except in four cases:

  • you give us affirmative consent for specific messages or events;
  • it is necessary for security purposes, such as investigating abuse;
  • it is required to comply with applicable law;
  • the data has been aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.

We transfer Google user data to service providers only as needed to operate these features (for example, hosting), and only under confidentiality obligations.

Latte’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect Google Calendar at any time in Latte’s calendar settings. This deletes the Google access tokens we store. You can also revoke Latte’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting your Latte account also revokes Latte’s access with Google and deletes the stored tokens.

8. International transfers

Our main infrastructure runs on Microsoft Azure in Switzerland and the European Union. Both are recognised as providing adequate data protection. Some of the providers listed above may process data in other countries, mainly the United States. Where that happens, we rely on an adequacy decision or on safeguards approved under the GDPR and the FADP. These include the EU–US and Swiss–US Data Privacy Frameworks, where the provider is certified, and Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask us for a copy of the relevant safeguards.

9. How long we keep data

  • Your account and content: for as long as your account exists. You can delete individual items at any time.
  • When you delete your account: your profile and content are erased across all Latte services, and your Google tokens are revoked. Billing and analytics records are anonymised. Encrypted backups are kept for up to 7 days and then overwritten.
  • Accounting records for purchases: for as long as commercial and tax law requires, which is generally 10 years. These records are kept separate from your account.
  • Security logs and diagnostics: for a limited period, as long as needed to investigate incidents.
  • Support messages: for as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy of it;
  • have it corrected;
  • have it deleted;
  • restrict its processing;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • receive it in a portable format (portability);
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting anything done before.

You can delete your account yourself in the app: open your Billing page and use Danger zone → Delete account. For any other request, including a copy of all your data in a machine-readable (JSON) format, email privacy@latte.study. We answer within 30 days, and we may ask you to confirm your identity.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Switzerland, this is the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC). In the EU or EEA, it is the authority in your country, listed by the EDPB. In the UK, it is the ICO. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

California and other US states: you have the right to know, delete and correct your data. We do not sell or share personal information as those laws define the terms, and we will not discriminate against you for using your rights.

11. Cookies and similar technologies

Latte uses browser storage (cookies and local storage) and similar technologies on mobile devices.

  • Strictly necessary: these keep you signed in, protect against cross-site request forgery, remember your language and your cookie choice, and keep the app fast and usable offline. They do not need consent.
  • Crash reports (web): when a page of the web app stops working, it sends Azure Application Insights one crash report: an error code, the error’s name, a cleaned-up message and stack trace, and the kind of page (never its full address). It contains no name, email address, account, session or device identifier and nothing you wrote, and it stores nothing on and reads nothing from your device: no cookie and no browser storage. Because it is needed to keep Latte working, it is sent whatever your cookie choice, on the basis of our legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)); you can object (see your rights). It is never used for analytics or combined with analytics data.
  • Analytics: these help us understand how Latte is used so we can improve it. On the web we use Hotjar and Azure Application Insights. In the mobile app we use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Microsoft Clarity. Where consent is required, you can accept or refuse these through the cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time by clearing it.

We use no advertising cookies and no cross-site tracking for ads. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary cookies, Latte may stop working properly.

12. Security

We protect your data with:

  • encryption in transit (TLS), including connections to our databases;
  • signed and encrypted sign-in tokens with short lifetimes, and keys that we rotate;
  • per-user encryption of billing records;
  • network isolation between our services;
  • multi-factor protection on administrative tools;
  • a tamper-evident audit log for billing.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will notify you and the competent authorities as the law requires.

13. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when Latte changes, and we will show the new effective date at the top. If we make an important change, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent again.
