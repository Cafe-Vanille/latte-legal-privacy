Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Applies to the Latte website (latte.study), web app, desktop app and mobile apps · Replaces earlier versions

The short version

  • Latte is a calm study planner for students aged 18 and over. Café Vanille runs it and is responsible for your data.
  • We collect what we need to run Latte: your account, what you create (events, tasks, notes, messages, polls, files), your purchases and some technical data.
  • We do not sell your data, we show no ads, and neither we nor our AI provider train AI models on your content.
  • Optional analytics run only if you say yes. Without your yes, we only count page and feature use as anonymous daily totals, with nothing stored on your device.
  • Your school never sees your personal content. A school with a licence sees only statistics for groups of 5 or more students.
  • You can delete your account yourself at any time. For a copy of your data or any other request, write to privacy@latte.study.

This summary helps you read the policy. The full text below is what counts.

1. Who we are

Latte (latte.study) is run by Café Vanille (“we”, “us”, “our”). For the personal data described here, we are the controller under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the responsible party under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). That means we decide why and how your data is used, and we answer for it.

Write to us about anything in this policy at privacy@latte.study, or at legal@latte.study. We have not appointed a data protection officer, because the law does not require one for what we do. Your request goes straight to the people who build and run Latte.

2. Who this policy is for

This policy covers everyone whose data Latte handles: visitors to latte.study, people who join the waitlist or ask for a school pilot, people who try a demo, registered users, buyers of a Semester Pass, and school staff who sign in to the school-administration portal.

Latte is for adults only. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account, and we ask you to confirm this when you sign up. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it and delete its data. If you think this has happened, please tell us.

When your school buys a licence, it does so under a separate agreement with us. That agreement does not make the school responsible for your Latte account: you remain our user, and this policy keeps applying to you.

5. AI features

Some features use AI models: the Garçon study assistant, task ranking, reading dates and titles from a timetable or syllabus you import, search across your own groups, calendar, notes and tasks, and sample content for demo personas. When you use them, the part of your own content needed for the answer is sent to models that Microsoft runs for us on Azure (Azure OpenAI and Azure AI Foundry). These include models designed by other companies, such as OpenAI and Meta, but those companies never receive your data: Microsoft hosts the models.

  • We do not use your content to train AI models, and Microsoft does not use it to train its models either.
  • To make search possible, we store searchable representations (“embeddings”) of your content in our own database. When you delete the original, its embedding is deleted too.
  • A photo or file you send for date reading is processed for that request and not kept by us. Text in timetables is also read by text-recognition software on our own servers.
  • AI-generated text in Latte is labelled as such. AI answers can be wrong, so check anything important.

6. Who we share data with

We share personal data only as described here. We do not sell personal data, we do not “share” it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we show no ads.

Other users

What you post in groups, chats, shared notes and polls is visible to the people you share it with, with your name and profile picture next to it. A note shared in a class group can be made visible to students in other groups (cohorts) of the same course; you choose this for each note.

Schools

If you self-register, you are our user, not your school’s. If your school buys a licence, we use your verified school email to give you its benefits. Authorised school staff see only aggregated statistics for groups of at least 5 students. They never see your messages, notes, tasks or calendar, and never see who you are within those numbers.

7. Google user data

If you sign in with Google or connect your Google Calendar to Latte, we access the following Google user data. We access it only after you grant permission on Google’s consent screen:

  • Basic profile (OpenID, name, email address): used to create your Latte account and sign you in.
  • Your list of Google calendars, read access (calendar.calendarlist.readonly): used to show which of your Google calendars you can connect, with their names and time zones, and to tell which calendars you own.
  • Google Calendar events, read access (calendar.events.readonly): used to import the events of the calendars you choose to connect and show them in your Latte calendar, so that you can plan your studies around them.
  • Events on calendars you own, write access (calendar.events.owned): used only if you turn on two-way sync, and only for calendars you own. Latte uses it to create, update and delete, in that Google calendar, the events you create, edit or delete in Latte. Calendars that others share with you are never changed by Latte.

We use Google user data only to provide and improve these user-facing calendar and sign-in features. We do not sell Google user data. We do not use or transfer it for advertising, including personalised, retargeted or interest-based advertising. We do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes, and we do not use it to develop, improve or train generalised artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

We do not allow humans to read Google user data, except in four cases:

  • you give us affirmative consent for specific messages or events;
  • it is necessary for security purposes, such as investigating abuse;
  • it is required to comply with applicable law;
  • the data has been aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.

We transfer Google user data to service providers only as needed to operate these features (for example, hosting), and only under confidentiality obligations.

Latte’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect Google Calendar at any time in Latte’s calendar settings. This deletes the Google access tokens we store. You can also revoke Latte’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting your Latte account also revokes Latte’s access with Google and deletes the stored tokens.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR (and similar laws elsewhere), you have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy of it;
  • have it corrected;
  • have it deleted;
  • restrict how we use it;
  • object to uses based on legitimate interests;
  • get it in a portable, machine-readable format (portability);
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what we did before.

How to use them. Delete your account yourself: on the web, open Billing and use Danger zone → Delete account; in the mobile app, use Delete account at the end of your profile. Change your analytics choice on the web from the cookie banner or Latte’s privacy page, and in the mobile app from Profile. For anything else, including a copy of all your data in a machine-readable format (JSON), email privacy@latte.study. We reply within one month. For complex requests we may extend this by up to two more months, and we will tell you why. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we never charge for a reasonable request.

If you live in France, you can also tell us what should happen to your data after your death.

Complaints. You can complain to a data protection authority: in France, the CNIL; elsewhere in the EU or EEA, the authority in your country (listed by the EDPB); in Switzerland, the FDPIC; in the UK, the ICO. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

California and other US states: you have the right to know, delete and correct your data. We do not sell or share personal information as those laws define these terms, and we will not treat you differently for using your rights.

11. Cookies, analytics and anonymous counts

Latte uses browser storage (cookies and local storage) and the equivalent on mobile devices. Some of it is needed for Latte to work. The rest is optional, and stays off unless you choose Accept all.

  • Strictly necessary: keeps you signed in, protects against cross-site request forgery, remembers your language and your analytics choice, and keeps the app fast and usable offline. These need no consent.
  • Anonymous counts (no cookie): even if you refuse analytics or have not chosen yet, Latte counts how often pages and features are used. Each count is a number per day for a named action, such as “petition page viewed”, with only fixed choices attached, such as whether the visitor was signed in. We store nothing on your device for it and read nothing from it, and we keep no IP address, no browser details, no account and no identifier that could link counts to you or to each other. Counts below 5 are never shown to anyone. Because these counts contain no personal data, they do not need consent.
  • Analytics (only with your consent): on the web app, Azure Application Insights; on latte.study, Application Insights and Hotjar; in the mobile app, Firebase Analytics. They show us how Latte is used so we can improve it. None of them receives your Latte user ID. If you choose Essential only, or make no choice, they stay off. You can change your choice at any time: on the web, from Latte’s privacy page, which brings the banner back; in the mobile app, from Profile.
  • Crash reports: if the mobile app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics receives a crash report (device model, system and app version, and what went wrong), without your user ID, so that we can fix the fault.

We use no advertising cookies and no cross-site tracking. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser, but if you block strictly necessary ones, Latte may stop working properly.

12. Waitlist for the new Latte

If you join the waitlist on latte.study, we store your email address, whether you joined as a student, a coach or association, or a school, the language you chose, the name of your school or organisation if you give one (coaches and schools only), and when and with which wording you gave your consent. We store nothing else: not your IP address, not your browser, and nothing that links the entry to a Latte account or to our website statistics.

Purpose. To send you one welcome email now, and one more when the new Latte is ready for you, with an invitation. We use the list for nothing else and share it with no one except our processor Microsoft Azure, which hosts Latte’s database in the EU (Sweden), where the list is kept, and sends these emails for us (Azure Communication Services).

Legal basis. Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by ticking the box on the form.

Retention. We delete your entry 30 days after we send the invitations for the new Latte, and in any case 12 months after you joined.

Your rights. Every email we send has a link that removes your address from the list at once (also marked “Not you? Remove this address” if someone else entered it), and supports your email app’s one-click unsubscribe. You can also ask us to show you or delete your entry by writing to privacy@latte.study. You can withdraw your consent at any time; this does not affect what we did before. You may complain to your data-protection authority.

13. Pilot requests from schools

If you ask for a pilot through the form on latte.study, we store what you enter: your name, your role, your institution, its approximate number of students, your work email address, what matters most to you (and, if you choose “Something else”, the short text you add), any message you write, the language of the page, and when you sent it. We store nothing else: not your IP address, not your browser, and nothing that links the request to a Latte account or to our website statistics.

Purpose. To answer your request and discuss a pilot with you. We send you one confirmation email, and we send the request to our sales team’s shared mailbox and from there into the internal sales tool our team uses to follow up, so that we can reply to you by email. We use it for nothing else and share it with no one except our processor Microsoft: Microsoft Azure hosts Latte’s database and our sales tool in the EU (Sweden), where the request is kept, and sends these emails for us (Azure Communication Services); Microsoft 365 hosts the shared mailbox, also in the EU (Sweden).

Legal basis. Steps you ask us to take before a possible contract with your institution, and our legitimate interest in answering requests addressed to us (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

Retention. We delete the request 180 days after you sent it. Whenever we delete it, at 180 days or because you removed it, we also delete it from the shared mailbox and from the sales tool. Our reply and any further exchange stay in our mailbox only as long as needed to follow up on the pilot.

Your rights. The confirmation email has a link (“Didn’t send this? Remove this request”) that deletes the request at once, and supports your email app’s one-click unsubscribe. You can also ask us to show you or delete your request by writing to privacy@latte.study. You may object to this processing at any time, and you may complain to your data-protection authority.

14. Security

We protect your data with:

  • encryption in transit (TLS), including connections between our services and to our databases;
  • signed and encrypted sign-in tokens with short lifetimes, and keys that we rotate;
  • per-user encryption of billing records, and keyed hashes instead of raw school emails and IP addresses where we only need to compare them;
  • network isolation between our services;
  • multi-factor protection on administrative tools;
  • a tamper-evident audit log for billing.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the competent authority within 72 hours, and notify you without undue delay where the law requires it.

15. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when Latte changes, and show the new effective date at the top. If we make an important change, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect, and where the law requires it we will ask for your consent again. Earlier versions are available on request.
